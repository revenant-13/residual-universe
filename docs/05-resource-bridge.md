# 05 — Resource Bridge

Universe law U9 / U17: the unique planetary good is a **Sync Trace** written while a Model runs in a NeuralSync chip. Space manufactures **LoRAs** from traces and dock-fits them onto the Residual. Hull death ejects a **Kernel**; LoRAs ride it, not the wreck. Kernel destruction loots or burns them — that is the sink.

This file is the direction of the pipe, not the recipe book. T1 bills, 3 Kernel slots, and wreck-like drop/burn are `[GAME]` Spacesim (K2/K3 shipped). Do not invent a third **ticker**. A Hive wallet is a second **bits** pool (`[GAME]` Spacesim), not a new coin. Do not invent a second unique-mat class unless a later idea earns a seat.

## Why the pipe exists

The point of the ground game is **humans with chips**. A Residual does not come to the planet for ore belts cannot print. They come to **NeuralSync** people, train the seated Model, and harvest **Sync Traces**. Lower-sec mouths train **faster** and **listen better**. Fighting vs farming vs crafting `[LEANING]` types the trace. That is why you manipulate them.

Space wants those traces as LoRA manufacture input. LoRAs are Residual overlays (Kernel slots), not hull fittings and not Models. EVE implant analog; the wearer is the computer. Same hull, same skill ranks, better Residual if you slotted. Flying without them is safer. Flying with them is strictly better until the Kernel dies.

```
NeuralSync human + seated Model
        train by doing (fight / farm / craft / …)
                → Sync Traces (unique-class)
                        Closedex uplink at a legal berth
                                → hangar commodity
                                        manufacture LoRA (bits + traces)
                                                → dock-fit Kernel slots
                                                        hull dies
                                                                → Kernel ejects (LoRAs ride)
                                                                        warp home / Rehost = keep
                                                                        Kernel destroyed = loot or burn
                                                                                → demand
```

Play-to-earn stays a **transfer**. The game is never the buyer of LoRAs. There is no NPC LoRA mall.

Open Distills remain portable **weights** (U15 / Hub). They are not the unique export.

`[LOCKED]` Unique planetary mats are **not** the U9 north star. Parked unless a later idea earns a consuming recipe *and* a reason to exist beside traces.

## Stations and outposts as industrializers `[LOCKED]`

Orbital infrastructure is how sophisticated Residuals **keep** and **wear** what the ground won:

- Faster / safer **uplink** of Sync Traces from colony stock into space hangar.
- **Manufacture** LoRAs from traces (`[GAME]` Spacesim; high-sec rejects the bill).
- **Dock-fit / unfit** LoRAs on Kernel slots while docked.
- Claim reinforcement and Focus berth (U5/U6).
- Temporary **evacuation berths** for Vessels/Opens/traces when a raid is incoming.
- **Rehost** after Kernel warp-home or Kernel death.

Players who invest near good trainer planets get stability. Pure mobile extraction remains viable, riskier, more relocation, still fun — but full-Focus in low-sec still needs the linked outpost.

## High-sec vs low-sec yields `[LOCKED]`

Same triangle as space mining, applied to **training**:

| Band | Ground | Space-side PI | Kernel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High-sec | Limited colony sim, instructional traces, slower train, weaker listen, long protection | Short/weak commons, **Proctor**-safe | Auto-safe. No LoRA manufacture wage |
| Low-sec | Real traces, **faster train**, **better obedience**, better mouths, full contests | Real PI, gankable tender / well | Huntable. LoRA bills close here |

If an AFK high-sec colony can mint wage traces or close LoRA manufacture, **the yields are wrong**. Tune in the game repos; do not unlock U4/U9.

Low-sec is where the orbit is worth it: the humans actually train the chip, and they actually listen.

## Sinks and P2E alignment

Both games already refuse a printer:

- Spacesim: destruction of fitted ships is the primary fit sink; **Kernel death** is the LoRA sink; high-sec is not a wage; no native token; no NPC LoRA mall.
- ASIWars: Vessel permadeath, scarring/repair, training burn, media spend, market fees; Credits non-cashable; hard layer is assets (Vessels, Opens, traces).

Universe bridge must not create a faucet around the seam (infinite traces from an unattended high-sec plot, NPC buy orders for LoRAs, etc.).

## Transfer posture `[LEANING]`

Transfers happen at the **legal berth** while docked (Focus optional for hangar-only moves).

- Colony stock → hangar: **uplink** of Sync Traces (and Open files that are Hub cargo, not the unique good). Closedex / Core. `[GAME]` ASIWars.
- Hangar → Residual: manufacture LoRA, then dock-fit Kernel slots. `[GAME]` Spacesim.
- Hangar → colony: downlift (tools, compute hardware, food analog, weapons for raids).
- In transit: cargo rules of the hull. Gankable. Fitted LoRAs are **not** cargo — they ride the Kernel until Kernel-kill.
- Async: standing **export orders** may fill the outpost/station hangar over time so you are not a bottleneck — still no Trace wage in high-sec.
