# 05 — Resource Bridge

Universe law U9: contested planetary extraction produces **unique / high-value inputs** that feed space industry. This file is the direction of the pipe, not the recipe book.

Exact ids, quantities, and invention bills stay `[GAME]` until both economies have a playable loop. Do not invent a third wallet.

## Why the pipe exists

Spacesim already needs inputs belts cannot print (T2 / special hulls and modules). v1 answered with **bloom windows**: stay in the ship, fight the well, haul PI.

The Residual Universe adds a second, deeper answer: **humans on the planet**, softly managed, rushing sites and rivals. That loop exists so destruction spectacle has somewhere to happen, and so space industry has goods worth contesting an orbit for.

```
Ground contests / colony production
        → unique / high-value mats, trained Models, (limited) Vessels
        → legal berth hangar (station or linked outpost)
        → space manufacture, invention, T2, extra-legal outpost jobs
        → fitted hulls that die
        → demand
```

Play-to-earn stays a **transfer**. The game is never the buyer.

## Two extractors, one Residual

| Path | Where | What it is | Wage? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Belts / rifts / wrecks | Space | Ore, salvage, modules, bits | High-sec no |
| Bloom windows / pads | Space, on-grid at planet | Light PI (biomass / sludge / refractory…) | High-sec no |
| **Colony + contested sites** | Ground instance | Unique / higher PI-class goods, Blanks, Models | High-sec no |

`[LOCKED]` High-sec colony output is instructional. It does not close T2 or clear USDC friction.

`[LEANING]` Blooms may remain as a **lighter dual source** so Spacesim planetary play is not gated on ASIWars shipping. They must not out-print the unique ground goods. Phasing blooms down later is allowed.

`[LOCKED]` Compact-outpost **residue / slag** stay space-side siphon products. They are not renamed planetary colony output. Outposts **industrialize and protect** planet goods; they are not the primary miners of planet-unique mats.

## What ground is allowed to emit

Direction only:

| Output | Role in space | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unique planetary mats | T2 / special bills, sinks | The point of U9. New ids need a consuming recipe **before** they extract (Spacesim faucet gate). |
| Limited common PI | Overlap with blooms | High-sec commons stay trash. |
| Models (weights) | Tradeable / loadable; not a turret stat | **Open** Models only (U15). Closed factory files never enter a hangar. Combat power stays ship fittings. |
| Vessels | Not space crew `[LOCKED]` | Humans do not board hulls as DPS. Evacuation hangar is **storage in transit**, then back to a colony or market. |
| Credits / bits | Soft tickers | Convert only at a legal berth. Rate `[OPEN]`. |

`[LOCKED]` Vessels are not gunners, capacitor, or drone bandwidth. The Residual flies the ship. Humans are a planetary (and market) layer.

## Stations and outposts as industrializers `[LOCKED]`

Orbital infrastructure is how sophisticated Residuals **keep** what the ground won:

- Faster / safer **uplift** from colony stock into space hangar.
- Processing / manufacture that **consumes** colony outputs (and residue, ore, datacores).
- Claim reinforcement and Focus berth (U5/U6).
- Temporary **evacuation berths** for Vessels/Models when a raid is incoming.
- Later: extra-legal T2 jobs that spend residue **and** planet-unique mats `[GAME]`.

Players who invest near good planets get stability. Pure mobile extraction remains viable, riskier, more relocation, still fun — but full-Focus in low-sec still needs the linked outpost.

## High-sec vs low-sec yields `[LOCKED]`

Same triangle as space mining:

| Band | Ground | Space-side PI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-sec | Limited colony sim, trash/instructional mats, long protection | Short/weak commons, CONCORD |
| Low-sec | Real unique mats, better Blanks, full contests | Real PI, gankable tender / well |

If an AFK high-sec colony can manufacture T2 or clear export friction, **the yields are wrong**. Tune in the game repos; do not unlock U4/U9.

## Sinks and P2E alignment

Both games already refuse a printer:

- Spacesim: destruction of fitted ships is the primary sink; high-sec is not a wage; no native token.
- ASIWars: Vessel permadeath, scarring/repair, training burn, market fees; Credits non-cashable; hard layer is assets.

Universe bridge must not create a faucet around the seam (infinite unique mats from an unattended high-sec plot, NPC buy orders for colony goods, etc.).

## Transfer posture `[LEANING]`

Transfers happen at the **legal berth** while docked (Focus optional for hangar-only moves).

- Colony stock → hangar: uplift. Time, capacity, maybe sludge/fuel analog `[OPEN]`.
- Hangar → colony: downlift (tools, compute hardware, food analog, weapons for raids).
- In transit: cargo rules of the hull. Gankable.
- Async: standing **export orders** may fill the outpost/station hangar over time so you are not a bottleneck — still no unique-mat wage in high-sec.
